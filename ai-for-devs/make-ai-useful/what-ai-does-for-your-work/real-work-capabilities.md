# Real Work Capabilities

---
## Content

For developers, generative AI works best when you give it real source material and ask for a result you can check.

It can help you:

- **Write in your voice:** turn notes into a code review comment, ticket update, design-doc summary, or release note.
- **Read what you do not have time to read:** summarize API docs, issue threads, incident notes, or long review discussions.
- **Turn data into a chart:** explain patterns in logs, metrics, flaky-test history, or spreadsheet exports.
- **Help with meetings:** draft an agenda, summarize notes you provide, or list follow-ups from a design review or retro.

The pattern is simple: provide context, get a first pass, verify the result.

The better the source material, the easier it is to review the output.
