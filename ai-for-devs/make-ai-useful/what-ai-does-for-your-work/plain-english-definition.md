# Plain English Definition

---
## Content

Here is the plain definition:

A system trained on huge amounts of content that produces new content from a description.

Older AI often sorted, predicted, or classified. Generative AI makes new material: text, summaries, charts, images, outlines, and code.

Without using the word "AI," you can say:

Software that creates a new draft, answer, image, or analysis from what you ask for.

For developer work, the key word is **new**. New output can be useful, but it has not been checked just because it looks complete.
