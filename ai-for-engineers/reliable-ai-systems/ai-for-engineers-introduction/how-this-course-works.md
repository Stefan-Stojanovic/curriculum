# How This Course Works

---
## Content

The lessons are short, but the decisions are real engineering decisions.

You will see scenarios like support summaries, search assistants, incident updates, document review, code generation, triage, and workflow automation. The point is not to memorize one architecture. The point is to learn how to judge AI behavior under constraints.

Expect to practice:

- spotting reliability gaps in a system proposal
- turning a vague AI idea into acceptance criteria
- choosing an eval, review gate, or fallback for a failure mode
- deciding when cost, latency, or review load changes the release plan

When an exercise asks you to connect an idea to your own work, use the comments to reason through a feature, incident, or workflow you know. If you want a second perspective, ask Enki AI in the comments to challenge your risk assessment.
